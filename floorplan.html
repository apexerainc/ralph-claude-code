Estimation Notes & Methodology

  • Primary references: Interior door frames (standard 6'-8" x 2'-8") used as the main scaling reference for all measurements.
  • Secondary references: Oak hardwood floor boards (standard 2-1/4" width) used to cross-check horizontal distances.
  • Tertiary references: Standard electrical outlet height (12" AFF), window proportions, and fireplace mantel dimensions.
  • Camera height: All photos taken at approximately 5 ft from floor level, consistent with stated conditions.
  • Accuracy: Estimated ±6" on room dimensions, ±2" on door/window widths. Perspective distortion from wide-angle phone cameras may affect measurements.
  • Chimney breast: Clearly visible projection from south wall with blue painter's tape at ceiling line. TV mounted on chimney face.
  • Orientation: "North" assigned to the window wall for reference; actual compass orientation unknown from photos.
  • Outlets: Positions are approximate - some may be obscured by furniture. Only outlets visible in photos are shown.
